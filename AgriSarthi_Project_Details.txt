AgriSarthi is an autonomous, low-cost smart agricultural monitoring rover 
designed to empower small to medium-scale Indian farmers with real-time 
field intelligence. It uses dual-microcontroller hardware, multi-modal 
environmental sensors, computer vision (YOLOv8 & OpenCV), and a 
cloud-connected web/mobile dashboard to provide continuous 24/7 crop 
health, soil parameter, and security monitoring.

3. THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE
================================================================================

Over 85% of Indian farmers lack access to precision monitoring tools. 
Key challenges in modern farming:

  1. LATE DETECTION
     Diseases, pests, and nutrient issues go unnoticed until significant 
     crop damage has occurred (30–40% foliage loss before manual detection).

  2. MANUAL MONITORING IS INFEASIBLE
     Not practical to physically inspect large fields regularly, especially
     during extreme heat, nighttime, or monsoons.

  3. IRRIGATION UNCERTAINTY
     Lack of real-time soil moisture data leads to over-irrigation or 
     under-irrigation, wasting water & reducing yield.

  4. CLIMATE RISKS
     Droughts, floods, heat waves, and erratic rainfall patterns threaten 
     crops with no early warning system in place.

  5. LIMITED CONNECTIVITY
     Remote farming areas often lack reliable internet and expert advisory 
     services, leaving farmers isolated.

  6. PROHIBITIVE COSTS
     Conventional solutions — tractors (₹6L+), agri-drones (₹4L+), and 
     imported agri-robots (₹2.5L–₹5L) — are unaffordable for smallholders.


4. OUR SOLUTION — AGRISARTHI
================================================================================

An autonomous rover platform that provides:

  • CONTINUOUS 24/7 FIELD MONITORING
    Autonomous rover collects real-time field data from multiple field zones.

  • EARLY DETECTION VIA EDGE AI
    AI analyzes camera feed and sensor data to detect disease, pest 
    intrusion, and environmental risks at the earliest stage.

  • WORKS IN LOW CONNECTIVITY
    On-device Edge AI (YOLOv8 on Raspberry Pi 4) reduces dependency on 
    internet. Syncs data when connection is available.

  • SMARTER RESOURCE USAGE
    Soil moisture intelligence supports precision irrigation and input 
    decisions, reducing water and chemical waste.

6. SENSOR & ACTUATOR SUITE
================================================================================

  • Soil Moisture Sensor — Capacitive sensor for real-time soil hydration
  • DHT22              — Ambient temperature (-40°C to 80°C) & relative
                          humidity (0–100% RH)
  • MQ-2 Gas Sensor    — Air quality, smoke, methane, and hazardous gas
                          detection in fields/greenhouses
  • Sound Level Sensor — Ambient noise & anomaly detection (decibel meter)
  • PIR Motion Sensor  — Passive Infrared motion detection for nocturnal
                          security & intruder alerts
  • HC-SR04 Ultrasonic — Real-time obstacle detection & collision avoidance
                          (3x array, 2cm–400cm range)
  • Camera Module      — Pi Camera V2 / HD USB 120° Wide FOV with live 
                          RTSP/WebRTC stream
  • Drivetrain         — 4WD High-Torque DC Geared Motors with L298N/BTS7960
                          motor driver modules
  • Power Supply       — 12V 6.0Ah LiFePO4/Li-Ion with 3S Smart BMS &
                          25W Solar Trickle Charging Panel


7. SOFTWARE & AI CAPABILITIES
================================================================================

7.1 Computer Vision & Object Detection
  • Model: Custom-trained YOLOv8n (Nano) optimized for Raspberry Pi edge
  • Detectable Classes:
      - Humans / Farm Workers
      - Stray Animals / Cattle / Pests
      - Field Hazards / Obstacles
      - Crop Disease & Weed Detection (Expansion Module)
  • Performance: Real-time inferencing @ 24+ FPS with bounding box 
    overlay & confidence scores

7.2 Autonomous Navigation
  • Autonomous Mode:
      - OpenCV-based row/path-following algorithm
      - HC-SR04 ultrasonic array for dynamic obstacle detection & stopping
      - Automatic waypointing & perimeter patrolling
  • Manual Remote Control Mode:
      - Low-latency WebSocket joystick/button control via web dashboard

7.3 Multi-Page Dashboard & Cloud Web Application
  • Architecture: React 19 + React Router DOM 7 multi-page application with 
    smooth routing and persistent top navigation. Clicking brand logo routes 
    directly to Home (/).
  • Dedicated 5-Page Structure:
      1. Home (/) — OTTO-style industrial hero, "Why AgriSarthi" 2-col 
         interactive accordion, live holographic telemetry preview, fleet 
         status ticker, rover lineup selector, and software autonomy cards.
      2. Rovers (/rovers) — Dedicated modular fleet showcase (Sentinel, 
         Weeder, Scout), interactive chassis schematic with sensor callouts, 
         spec badges, and deep-dive hardware modal.
      3. Mission Control (/mission-control) — Interactive pilot-seat HUD 
         simulator with simulated camera stream, real-time YOLOv8 bounding 
         boxes, virtual drive controls, sensor dials, and emergency triggers.
      4. Services (/services) — Zero-CapEx Robots-as-a-Service (RaaS) tiers, 
         interactive Farm ROI & Payback Engine (savings vs. manual labor), 
         and Dual-Brain hardware architecture specs.
      5. About (/about) — Prestigious awards & field proof, 4-slide investor 
         pitch deck carousel, founding team cards (Ayush Matkar & Atharva 
         Pachpol with official portraits), and direct WhatsApp & lab contact form.
  • Design & Aesthetics: Clean 5-zone agricultural & industrial color gamut 
    eliminating white-page fatigue, industrial typography hierarchy (Outfit, 
    Space Grotesk, Space Mono), and scroll-triggered pop animations.
